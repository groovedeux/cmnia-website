We Don't Just Sell Insurance.

We Are Here to Protect Your Way of Life.

At Central MN Insurance we care about your dreams and your plans, and we want to help you make them a reality, without getting blindsided by the many challenges we face in life.

Our insurance agents strive to make sure you are getting the coverage that is right for you. Once the right coverage is found, policies are annually renewed to do our best to make sure that the coverage continues to fit your needs. Our agents are licensed and work hard to stay updated on the latest insurance information by continually completing education credits every year.

Surprises in life aren't always a good thing. At Central MN Insurance, we are here to help. From your 16-year-old backing into the garage door, to protection in case of serious illness or a house fire, we have what you need to protect your finances and assets, and get you through life without worrying about what might be around the corner. For more information, look at our products page for a complete description of the types of insurance we offer.
